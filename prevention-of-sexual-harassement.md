## Summary of POSH Guidelines

### 1. Behaviours That Cause Sexual Harassment:
* **Verbal:** Offensive or sexist jokes, sexual innuendos, inappropriate comments about body/clothing, spreading rumors, and **repeated unwelcome requests for dates**.
* **Visual:** Displaying or transmitting sexually explicit posters, pictures, cartoons, emails, messages, or screensavers.
* **Physical:** Unwelcome touching, forced hugs, kissing, blocking movement, or suggestive gestures.
* **Quid Pro Quo & Hostile Environment:** Demanding sexual favors in exchange for job benefits/promotions (Quid Pro Quo), or engaging in pervasive conduct that creates an intimidating or offensive work environment.

---

### 2. Actions to Take If Facing or Witnessing Harassment:
1. **Directly tell the person to stop:** Clearly inform the individual that their behavior is unwelcome and must cease immediately.
2. **Document all details:** Maintain a written log of dates, times, specific actions/words, and witnesses.
3. **Report to HR / ICC:** Submit a formal written complaint to your supervisor, HR, or the Internal Complaints Committee (ICC).
4. **Duty for witnesses:** Report any observed harassment to HR or the ICC.
5. **Protection:** Complaints are handled confidentially and are strictly protected against retaliation.
